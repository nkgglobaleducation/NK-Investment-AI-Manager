⚖️ Strategy Rules & Capital Execution

1. Monday/Tuesday (50% + 50% Core): Enter at 09:30 AM instead of 09:15 AM to eliminate ~20 bps in opening spread friction. Exit on Wednesday at 10:30–11:30 AM (or at Wed close fallback).

2. Thursday 3% Dip Rule: Intraday drops ≥3% are rare (~3–6% of sessions) but provide high-asymmetry bounce opportunities. Enter at next bar open.

3. Friday 3% Target Exit: Holding 3 to 5 days increases +3% target probability from 9.4% to 47.3%. Carry forward unresolved positions if target is not hit on Friday.

4. All Statutory Charges Included: STT (0.001%), GST (18%), SEBI turnover, stamp duty, DP charges, and brokerage are factored in.
