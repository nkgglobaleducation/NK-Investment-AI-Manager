6. AI CALL ↔ RATIONALE CONSISTENCY
========================================================

Implement deterministic validation.

BUY:
Rationale must contain a meaningful positive reason.

HOLD:
Rationale should indicate that the investment thesis remains acceptable but there is insufficient reason to materially increase or reduce.

SELL ON RALLY:
Rationale must identify a meaningful reason to reduce exposure, such as:
- valuation
- deteriorating growth
- cyclical risk
- weakening fundamentals
- inferior risk/reward

EXIT NOW:
Rationale must identify a serious negative thesis-breaking issue.

Do NOT allow:

EXIT NOW
+
"Strong fundamentals and attractive long-term prospects"

Likewise do not allow:

BUY
+
"Severe balance-sheet deterioration"

If mismatch occurs:
- regenerate the response
OR
- downgrade to a logically consistent CALL where appropriate.

Do not blindly change the CALL with hard-coded financial rules.

========================================================
7. ALT SYSTEM — MAJOR IMPROVEMENT
========================================================

ALT is OPTIONAL.

NEVER force an ALT.

ALT should be populated only when:

1. CALL is EXIT NOW or SELL ON RALLY
2. a genuinely better replacement exists
3. the replacement is a valid listed stock
4. the replacement is business/sector/theme comparable
5. replacement is NOT itself EXIT NOW
6. replacement is NOT itself SELL ON RALLY
7. replacement is not simply a generic favourite stock
8. there is sufficient data to justify the replacement

Otherwise:

ALT = ""

========================================================
8. CROSS-STOCK ALT VALIDATION
========================================================

This is extremely important.

AI analysis is performed across many stocks.

Therefore ALT validation must consider the COMPLETE analysis batch.

Example:

A → ALT = B

If:

B → EXIT NOW

or

B → SELL ON RALLY

then:

A → B is INVALID.

Do not save it.

Regenerate another ALT or leave ALT blank.

Also detect:

A → B
B → A

and unnecessary chains such as:

A → B
B → C
C → A

Do not allow circular replacement logic.
